## Diagnosis

The trained v5 produces well-formed answers (right format, in-vocab,
no errors / no unparseable). It's just choosing wrong answers, e.g.
gold=no -> pred=yes. So the splicer is wiring features through; the
spliced features are just less useful to the LLM than what vanilla
Qwen's encoders give directly.

Most likely cause: VIB reparameterization adds noise even at beta=0
(z = mu + sigma * eps). Plus the random-init MutualCrossAttention
between encoder and LLM is mixing features in ways the frozen LLM
wasn't pretrained to read. 100 steps of LoRA isn't enough to
compensate for arbitrary perturbations of encoder outputs.

## Open architectural questions for tomorrow

1. **Are bottlenecks needed at all?** Original VIB / C-MIB motivation
   was compressing raw signals. Qwen's encoders already give compact
   features. Q-Former-style bottlenecks made sense for BLIP-2 because
   the LLM expected a fixed small token budget — Qwen3-Omni accepts
   variable-length encoder outputs natively.

2. **Where should the IB live?**
   - Option A: at encoder's last layer output (unimodal regularization)
   - Option B: at LoRA layers inside the LLM (regularize where info flows)
   - Option C: only on unimodal paths, no joint VIB
   - Option D: ditch IB, keep just MutualCrossAttention (but then it's
     not C-MIB anymore — losing the whole story)

3. **Is fusion the right operation?** MutualCrossAttention is one choice.
   Alternatives: residual cross-attention (preserves input by default),
   simple concat, learned gating.

4. **What did v1/v2 (Vicuna era) do that worked?** Worth re-reading
   `model/legacy/av_model_v1.py` and v2 to see if the design was sound
   or if Vicuna was just less sensitive to perturbation than Qwen.

5. **Could we initialize VIBs as near-identity?** mu = input, log_sigma
   biased to -inf so reparameterization noise vanishes. Train would
   start at baseline behavior and learn perturbations from there.

6. **Aux head leakage concern** (raised but never investigated): aux
   heads see pre-fusion z_v / z_a. The same z_v / z_a (via fusion +
   VIB_j) reach the LLM. Aux loss pushes z to encode the answer first
   token; LLM gets answer for free. Could explain part of the nll drop.
